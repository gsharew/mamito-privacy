1. Who we are

FidelLabs ("FidelLabs", "we", "us") is a social media management service that lets businesses and individuals plan, schedule and publish content to multiple social media platforms from one place. The service is available at https://mamito.fidellab.com.

The service is operated by FidelLab, [Street address], [City], Ethiopia. For the purposes of data protection law, we are the controller of the personal information described in this policy.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account information: your name, email address, company or workspace name, and password. Passwords are stored in hashed form, never as plain text.
  • Content you create: post text, images, videos and other media you upload, drafts, comments, tags, signatures, schedules and settings.
  • Team information: email addresses of people you invite to your workspace and the roles you give them.
  • Support messages: anything you send us when you contact us.

Information from sign-in providers

If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google to create and secure your account.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical data: IP address, browser type and device information, collected when you register and use the service to keep your account secure and prevent abuse.
  • Usage and error data: information about how the service performs, such as errors that occur when a post fails to publish, so we can fix problems.

Payment information

If you buy a paid plan, payments are processed by our payment provider. We receive your billing status and plan details, but we do not receive or store your full card number.

3. Connected social media accounts

When you connect a social media account (for example Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest or Threads), you are sent to that platform to approve access. With your approval, we receive and store:

  • Access tokens and refresh tokens that let us act on the account on your behalf.
  • The account or page ID, name, username and profile picture.
  • For business pages and channels you choose, the information needed to publish to them.
  • Post performance data, such as likes, comments, views and follower counts, when you use analytics features.

Each connected account belongs to your workspace. Other FidelLabs users cannot see or use your connected accounts unless you invite them to your workspace.

We only use this access to do what you ask: publish and schedule your posts, show you previews, display your analytics, and keep your connection active. We do not post anything you did not create or schedule.

Some platforms, such as Bluesky, WordPress or Medium, ask you to enter an app password or API key instead of signing in. These credentials are stored securely and used only to publish your content.

5. How we share information

We share information only in these situations:

  • With the social platforms you connect, to publish your content and retrieve your analytics. Their use of your data is governed by their own privacy policies.
  • With service providers that help us run FidelLabs, under agreements that limit how they use the data. These include cloud hosting and storage (Google Cloud), email delivery, payment processing, error monitoring, and AI providers (such as OpenAI) when you use AI features.
  • With members of your workspace, who can see the workspace's posts, media and connected accounts according to the role you give them.
  • For legal reasons, when required by law or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users or the public.
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger or sale, in which case this policy will continue to apply to your information.

6. Google and YouTube data

FidelLabs's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

If you connect a YouTube channel, FidelLabs uses YouTube API Services to upload and manage videos you choose and to show channel and video statistics. By connecting YouTube, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

You can revoke FidelLabs's access to your Google account at any time from your Google security settings. When access is revoked or you disconnect the channel, we delete the stored tokens for that channel.

7. Facebook and Instagram data

If you connect a Facebook Page or Instagram professional account, we use the permissions you approve only to publish your content, list the pages and accounts you manage so you can choose one, and show engagement data for your posts. We do not use Meta platform data for advertising, sell it, or share it with data brokers.

You can remove FidelLabs's access at any time in Facebook under Settings & privacy > Settings > Business integrations, or by disconnecting the account inside FidelLabs. See Deleting your data for how to request deletion.

8. Cookies

We use a small number of cookies that are needed for the service to work:

  • Sign-in cookie: keeps you logged in securely.
  • Workspace cookie: remembers which workspace you are using.
  • Preference cookies: remember your theme (light or dark), language and timezone.

9. How long we keep data

  • Account and content data: kept while your account is active.
  • Connected account tokens: kept until you disconnect the account, revoke access on the platform, or delete your FidelLabs account.
  • After you delete your account: we delete or anonymize your personal information within 30 days, except where we must keep it longer for legal, tax or security reasons. Backups are overwritten within [90] days.

10. Deleting your data

You can delete your data in any of these ways:

  • Disconnect a social account: open the channel's menu in FidelLabs and choose to disconnect or delete it. Its tokens are removed from our systems.
  • Delete your account: go to Settings and delete your account. This removes your account, workspace content and connected accounts.
  • Ask us: email [privacy@your-domain.com] from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my data". We will confirm and complete the request within 30 days.

Deleting data in FidelLabs does not delete posts that were already published on social media. You can remove those directly on each platform.

11. Security

We protect your information with measures that include encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, access controls that keep each workspace's data separate, and restricted access to production systems. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you as required by law.
